# E-commerce stores and marketplaces

Read the web file as well. Consumer, tax, payment and marketplace rules differ by country; verify each at the official source for every market sold into.

- [ ] **ECOM1 [CRITICAL] Place real orders end to end.** Cover browsing, cart, checkout, payment, confirmation, fulfilment, shipping notice, delivery, return and refund, for each payment method. Why: this is the whole business. Verify: complete a real order and a real refund for each payment method.
- [ ] **ECOM2 [CRITICAL] Keep card data out of your systems, or meet the card industry standard.** Use the provider's hosted fields or checkout so card numbers never touch your server. Why: handling card data directly brings the full payment card security standard into scope. Verify: confirm with the provider which self-assessment applies, and check that no card data appears in logs or the database.
- [ ] **ECOM3 [CRITICAL] Verify prices, taxes, shipping costs and discounts.** Test each tax region, shipping zone, currency and coupon, including rounding. Why: pricing errors are honoured at a loss or cause disputes. Verify: compare calculated totals with hand calculations for a set of sample orders.
- [ ] **ECOM4 [HIGH] Keep inventory accurate.** Prevent overselling when two customers buy the last item, and release stock from abandoned checkouts. Why: selling stock that does not exist means cancellations. Verify: two simultaneous purchases of the last unit result in one order.
- [ ] **ECOM5 [CRITICAL] Publish the consumer information each market requires.** Seller identity, total price including taxes and fees, delivery times, returns, cancellation and complaint routes. Why: consumer law in most markets requires specific pre-purchase information. Verify: the required items are visible before payment. Needs: A2.
- [ ] **ECOM6 [HIGH] Define and test returns, refunds and exchanges.** Why: unclear returns handling causes chargebacks. Verify: process a return through the system as a customer and as staff.
- [ ] **ECOM7 [HIGH] Set up fraud controls and a chargeback process.** Enable the provider's fraud screening, set rules for risky orders and decide who answers disputes. Why: new stores are targeted for card testing. Verify: a test high-risk order is flagged, and a dispute notification reaches a person.
- [ ] **ECOM8 [HIGH] Send order emails and messages.** Confirmation, payment receipt, shipping with tracking, delivery, refund and abandoned cart where consent allows. Why: customers expect a record of every step. Verify: trigger each one.
- [ ] **ECOM9 [HIGH] Check product data.** Titles, descriptions, images, variants, prices, stock, weights and categories, with no test products. Why: wrong product data is the main source of returns. Verify: review a sample of products and search for test entries.
- [ ] **ECOM10 [HIGH] Confirm shipping and fulfilment integrations.** Rates, labels, tracking, serviceable areas and cash on delivery where offered. Why: an order that cannot be shipped is a refund. Verify: create a real shipment.
- [ ] **ECOM11 [MEDIUM] Add product structured data and a product feed.** Why: enables rich results and shopping listings. Verify: the testing tool reports no errors.
- [ ] **ECOM12 [HIGH] Check product compliance and restricted goods.** If: selling regulated products. Confirm labelling, safety, age and import rules for each product type and market. Why: selling restricted goods brings penalties and payment account closure. Verify: each product category is checked against the regulator's and the payment provider's lists.
- [ ] **ECOM13 [CRITICAL] Onboard and pay sellers correctly.** If: the product is a marketplace. Verify seller identity as the payment provider and the law require, hold and split funds through a compliant flow, and handle seller tax reporting. Why: moving money between parties is regulated. Verify: complete a seller onboarding, a sale and a payout, and confirm the flow with the payment provider.
- [ ] **ECOM14 [HIGH] Moderate listings and handle disputes between buyers and sellers.** If: the product is a marketplace. Why: the platform is held responsible for what is sold on it. Verify: a reported listing reaches a reviewer, and a dispute follows a written process.
- [ ] **ECOM15 [MEDIUM] Test the store under a sale.** Why: a promotion can bring many times the normal traffic to checkout. Verify: load test the cart and checkout path. Needs: N2.
